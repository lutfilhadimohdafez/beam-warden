Why Isolation Forest
Unsupervised anomaly detection for WiFi telemetry

Isolation Forest isolates anomalies by random partitioning. Anomalies are isolated in fewer splits because they are rare and distinct, yielding a short average path length. The score formula: s = 2^(-E(h)/c(n)) where E(h) is the mean path length across all trees and c(n) = 2H(n-1) - 2(n-1)/n is the average path length of unsuccessful search in BST. Score > 0.5 = normal, < 0.5 = anomaly.
