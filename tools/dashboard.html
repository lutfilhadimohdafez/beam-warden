ⓘ HOW TO USE THE LEARNING TAB
The detector catches the attack by its on-air noise: the beam-spoof injects forged feedback frames pretending to be your phone, so those frames roughly double on air. You just teach it this network’s normal rate first. Three steps:
  1. Where am I? — tap Home, Event, or Office. Each place keeps its own normal, so you only learn once per place.
  2. Learn my normal Wi-Fi — with the attack OFF and the Wi-Fi in its usual use, press the big button once. It listens for a few seconds and remembers the normal feedback rate.
  3. Live Check — after that it just shows NORMAL (green) or ATTACK (red). Red means the feedback rate spiked above this place’s normal.
Needs the live BFI feed running (BFI-Storm panel active). “Show details” reveals the raw frames/sec and the learned threshold.
